☎️ Communicating with me

📺 Channels

  • Slack is the best way to reach me
  • Send me a DM or tag me where you need input
  • For nuanced discussions, I’m happy to jump on a quick Zoom or huddle

🕖 Time

  • I work 9–5 Eastern
  • My calendar is up to date. Please ask before booking over an existing meeting
  • I try to keep the later part of my week light on meetings for focus time
03

1:1s with direct reports

  • My primary goal is to support you — as such I leave the agenda largely up to you.
  • Our 1:1s can have as much or as little structure as you would like.
  • When requesting feedback, I can provide the best feedback if you’re specific.

    A better feedback request

    Specific

    “Could you give me feedback on ___ MR, I want to know what you think about how I designed ___ function”

    Too broad

    “Could you give me feedback on MRs”

  • Anything sensitive we discuss is within our circle of trust.
  • It’s important to me to have personal relationships with the people I work with. I love hearing what you have going on outside work.
